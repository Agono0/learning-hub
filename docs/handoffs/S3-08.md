## What was delivered

- **`packages/core/src/tasks/reminders.ts`** (new): per-task reminder
  planner reusing the global reminder patterns (`isQuietHours` /
  `extractTimeHHMM` from `packages/core/src/reminders`):
  - `taskReminderSchema`/`TaskReminder` mirroring `task_reminders`
    (S3-01): `at_time` (fixed ISO `remindAt`) and `relative`
    (`offsetMs` from the due moment; `remindAt` null at write time,
    resolved at plan time), `exactAlarm` flag,
  - `createTaskReminder` (cross-field refine: at_time ⇔ remindAt,
    relative ⇔ offsetMs) + `updateTaskReminder` (immutable),
  - `planTaskReminders(task, reminders, quiet, now)` →
    `{ planned, unresolved }`: resolved fire times, past flagging for the
    scheduler to retire, quiet-hours flagging under the GLOBAL quiet
    policy (same window as learning reminders), human labels
    ("Due in 30 min: …" / "Overdue by 15 min: …"), deterministic sort;
    reminders that can never fire (relative on a due-less task, bad
    timestamps) land in `unresolved` with the reason — no silent vanish,
  - `supportsExactAlarms(platform)`: exact-alarm path is Android-only —
    native side maps it to `AlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle` and
    must degrade to inexact when `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` is denied
    (documented for the O1-13-era plugin work + docs/native.md),
  - `validateTaskReminder` (type coherence + parseable timestamps).
- **`packages/core/src/tasks/index.ts`**: re-exports the module.
- **`packages/core/tests/tasks-reminders.test.ts`** (new): refine rules,
  at_time + relative resolution math (due 14:00Z − 30 min = 13:30Z),
  overdue labels, due-less unresolved reasons, foreign-task filtering,
  quiet-hours on/off flagging, exact-alarm capability matrix, validation.
