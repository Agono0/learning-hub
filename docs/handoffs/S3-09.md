## What was delivered

- **`packages/core/src/tasks/short-syntax.ts`** (new): SP's add-task-bar
  grammar, pure (dates resolve against an injected `now`):
  - Grammar: `#tag` (deduped, case preserved), `+project` (last wins),
    estimate chains `10m` / `1h` / `1h30m` (≤ 50 h cap, `parseEstimateMs`
    - `ESTIMATE_MAX_MS` exported for S3-62), due dates `@today` `@tom`
      `@tomorrow` `@+N` `@YYYY-MM-DD` (`resolveDateWord`; locale-soup forms
      like `12/24` deliberately unsupported), deadlines `!date`,
      priorities `p1..p3` (p1 = high per S3-64), repeats `@every 1w` /
      `@every d` (`@every` + cadence is a two-token lookahead; the first
      occurrence becomes the due date unless an explicit `@date` anchors it),
      and `"…"` quoting that protects text from tokenization.
  - Honest failures: unknown dates and bad cadences never abort the parse —
    they are dropped from the title and reported in `warnings`
    ("@someday is not a date I understood…"); bare prefixes being typed
    (`@`, `#`) vanish from the title instead of becoming words.
  - Autocomplete model: `tokenizeShortSyntax` → tokens with
    kind/offsets (quoted spans excluded from classification; estimate
    classification is SHAPE-based so `51h` warns instead of silently
    becoming title text); `suggestAt(input, caret)` → the suggestion
    context (`kind`, `trigger`, partial `query`) for `@ # + !` prefixes,
    `@every` cadences, and partial estimate chains — S3-68 renders it.

- **`packages/core/tests/tasks-short-syntax.test.ts`** (new): 30+ exhaustive
